Nationwide Delivery Services

DAHAR Logistics offers comprehensive domestic shipping solutions across Nigeria. Whether you need same-day delivery within Lagos or next-day service to any state, we've got you covered.

Service Features

  • Same Day Delivery: Within Lagos metropolis for shipments booked before 12 PM
  • Next Day Delivery: To all major cities across Nigeria
  • Door-to-Door Service: We pick up from your location and deliver directly to the recipient
  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor your shipment every step of the way
  • Insurance Coverage: Protect your valuable cargo with optional insurance
  • Proof of Delivery: Get confirmation with signature and photo proof
